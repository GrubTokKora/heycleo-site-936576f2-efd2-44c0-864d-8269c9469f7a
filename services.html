Pricing info

What affects the final price?

Our starting prices cover most small to medium dogs. A few things can affect the final cost.

Dog size
Small, medium, large and giant breeds are priced differently. See our full price guide below.
Coat condition
Matted or heavily knotted coats take longer to work through safely and may incur an additional fee.
Breed & style
Breed-specific cuts and intricate styles require more time and expertise. We'll advise upfront.
Add-ons
Dental care, de-shedding treatments and conditioning add-ons are priced separately.

Final prices vary by breed, coat condition and size. Cash always welcome.

Good to know

Salon policies

A few things to keep in mind before your dog's visit, so every groom goes smoothly for everyone.

Matted Coats

Badly matted dogs will need a short, all-over clip for their comfort and safety. Final pricing is confirmed at drop-off based on the groomer's assessment of your dog's coat condition.

Aggressive / Reactive Dogs

We love every dog, but safety comes first. Dogs showing aggressive behaviour may need to be muzzled, have grooming stopped early, or be declined for future bookings.

Vaccinations

All dogs must be up to date on core vaccinations and flea/tick prevention. Proof may be requested at drop-off.

Cancellations & No-Shows

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations, no-shows and late arrivals will incur a fee.

Health & Age

Please let us know of any health issues, injuries or sensitivities beforehand. Senior or unwell dogs may need a modified groom.

Drop-off & Pick-up

Dogs must be collected within 30 minutes of the finish time. Late pick-ups may incur a waiting fee. We'll always give you an estimated pick-up time at drop-off.

We treat every dog that comes through our doors like our own. If you have any questions about our services or policies, our friendly team is always happy to help — just ask!
