Our story

Born from a love of dogs and community

"We treat your pet like our very own — because to us, they're not just a client. They're family."

Pooch Professional Grooming was born from a desire to provide a dedicated, personalised local service for like-minded pet owners right here in Altona North. We understand that busy lifestyles often mean your pet may not get the care and attention they need — and that's exactly where we come in.

Whether it's a routine seasonal groom or a special treat, our team is ready to fulfil your pet's every grooming need. We know that each dog has their own individual personality — our groomers take the time to understand your dog so their style truly shines through.

  • ✓ Gentle, calm handling — no cage drying, ever
  • ✓ Premium dog-safe products only
  • ✓ Fully insured, health and safety first
  • ✓ Expert grooming advice, always free

Meet the team

The people behind the pampering

Each team member is chosen not just for their grooming skills, but for their genuine love of animals. It shows in every wag at pick-up.

Alisha

Groomer

Alisha has spent 8 years perfecting her craft and still gets excited about every single groom. Passionate about animal welfare and coat health, she combines technical skill with a gentle, caring approach that keeps dogs relaxed and owners coming back.

Jane

Groomer

Jane has been grooming for 4 years and loves every minute of it. Whether it's a tiny Chihuahua or a giant Bernese Mountain Dog, she gives every pup the same care, patience and attention. If there's a dog in the room, she's happy.

Irene

Groomer

If dogs could choose their own groomer, they'd all pick Irene. With over 10 years of experience, she has an extraordinary way with animals — calm, patient and completely in tune with every pup she meets. She specialises in small breeds and has a gift for even the most nervous dogs. They always leave wagging their tail.
