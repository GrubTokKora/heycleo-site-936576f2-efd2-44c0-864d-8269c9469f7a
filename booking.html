Your visit

What to expect

First time with us? Here's how a visit to Pooch Professional Grooming goes — from booking to pick-up.

1

Book your slot

Choose your service and pick a time that suits you online, by phone or by email. Same-week spots are often available.

2

Drop off your pup

Arrive at 3 Misten Avenue. Let us know of any sensitivities, health conditions or if your dog is nervous — we'll prepare accordingly.

3

We work our magic

Most grooms take 2–4 hours. We'll give you an estimated pick-up time at drop-off and call if anything comes up.

4

Pick up a happy pup

Come back to a freshly groomed dog, looking and smelling wonderful — and wagging their tail on the way out.

Before you come in

  • ✓Give your dog a short walk beforehand so they arrive calm and relaxed.
  • ✓Make sure they've had a toilet break before drop-off.
  • ✓Let us know of any health conditions, allergies or skin sensitivities when booking.
  • ✓First visit? Bring vaccination records — we may ask to see them at drop-off.

Absolutely. We work with anxious and nervous dogs all the time — it's one of the things we do best. We take things at their pace, use gentle handling techniques, and never force a dog into anything that causes distress. Just let us know when you book so we can prepare.
Just your dog! If they have a favourite toy or blanket for comfort, you're welcome to bring it. Vaccination records will be requested for first-time visits.

Most dogs benefit from a professional groom every 6–8 weeks. Long-coated or curly breeds — Poodles, Doodles, Shih Tzus — need it every 4–6 weeks to prevent matting. Short-coated breeds can go longer. Our team will happily advise the right schedule for your dog at your first visit.
