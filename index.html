Our story

We're dog people,
through and through

Pooch Professional Grooming was built on one simple belief — every dog deserves patience, love and a whole lot of care. We started as a family business to give Altona North pet owners a place they could genuinely trust.

We know your dog is family. Whether it's their very first groom or a regular seasonal spruce-up, we make every visit calm, kind and tail-waggingly good. Our groomers take the time to understand your dog's individual personality — so their style truly shines through.

  • ✓ Gentle, calm handling — no cage drying, ever

Before you visit

Questions we hear
all the time

If yours isn't here, call us — we're happy to chat.

Most dogs benefit from a groom every 6–8 weeks. Long-coated or curly breeds — Poodles, Doodles, Shih Tzus — need it every 4–6 weeks to prevent matting. Short-coated breeds can go longer. We're happy to recommend a schedule at your first visit.
Most full grooms take 2–4 hours depending on your dog's breed, size and coat condition. We'll give you an estimated pick-up time when you drop off. We never rush — your dog's comfort comes first.
Absolutely. We work with anxious and nervous dogs all the time — it's one of the things we do best. We take things at their pace, use gentle handling techniques, and never force a dog into anything that causes distress. Just let us know when you book so we can prepare.
We use premium, pet-safe shampoos and conditioners suited to each dog's coat type and skin. We stock Australian-made products wherever possible. If your dog has a known allergy or sensitivity, just let us know and we'll find the right product for them.
Give your dog a little exercise beforehand so they arrive calm and relaxed. Make sure they've had a toilet break before drop-off. If your dog has any health conditions, allergies or skin sensitivities, please let us know when booking so we can use the right products.
